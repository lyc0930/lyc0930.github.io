Biography

LUO Yanchen is currently a Research Assistant Professor with the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, under the supervision of Prof. Jian Huang. Before that, he was a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Xiangnan He and Prof. Xiang Wang, where he also received his B.Sc. degree from the School of the Gifted Young. His current research focuses on AI4Science, Agent4Science, and AI4Science Safety, including foundation and generative models for molecules and proteins, autonomous scientific agents for hypothesis generation and experiment planning, and robust, uncertainty-aware deployment of scientific AI systems.
