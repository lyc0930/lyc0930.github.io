Research Interests

My current research focuses on AI4Science, Agent4Science, and AI4Science Safety. I work on foundation and generative models for scientific discovery (especially molecules and proteins), scientific agents for autonomous hypothesis generation and experiment planning, and risk-aware scientific AI systems with robustness and uncertainty estimation.
